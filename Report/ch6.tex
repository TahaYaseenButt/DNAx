\section{Specialized National Software Modules}
The ecosystem comprises four specialized operational platforms:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{DNAx Signet\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (National Bio-Foundry Module)}: Operates inside secure central research laboratories. Performs thermodynamic modeling, in silico BLAST homology checks, primer/probe optimization, and cryptographic QR token generation.
    \item \textbf{DNAx Manufacturing\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Industrial Integration Module)}: Deployed on factory-floor terminals. Interfaces directly with micro-metering pumps on automated spray machinery to dispense exact taggant formulas and bind them to physical product serials.
    \item \textbf{DNAx Vendor\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Supply Chain Tracking Module)}: Deployed on handheld mobile terminals for customs officers, distributors, and logistics checkpoints. Enforces biometric verification, scans product QR codes, and logs timestamped GPS coordinates to create an unbroken chain of custody.
    \item \textbf{DNAx Forensics\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Enforcement Agency Module)}: Dedicated to national forensic institutes and security agencies. Ingests raw multi-channel qPCR telemetry, deconvolutes the 4-probe fluorophore combination, verifies authenticity against the central database, and generates cryptographically signed legal certificates.
\end{enumerate}
