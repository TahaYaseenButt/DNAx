\section{Application Modules}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{DNAx Signet\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Research Module)}: Operates in central laboratories for sequence generation, SantaLucia thermodynamic modeling, local BLAST screening, and specification dossier export.
    \item \textbf{DNAx Manufacturing\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Production Module)}: Deployed in manufacturing plants. Interfaces with spray machinery micro-metering pumps to dispense exact taggant formulas and log batch issuance.
    \item \textbf{DNAx Vendor\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Supply Chain Module)}: Deployed on mobile devices for customs officers and distributors. Verifies product QR codes and logs timestamped GPS tracking events.
    \item \textbf{DNAx Forensics\textsuperscript{\textcopyright} (Verification Module)}: Used by forensic laboratories. Ingests multi-channel qPCR telemetry, deconvolutes the 4-probe fluorophore combination, verifies authenticity against the database, and issues verification certificates.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
The successful completion of this proof-of-concept establishes a complete, domestic bio-molecular tagging capability:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{100\% In-House Technology}: Domestic control over software algorithms, biochemical synthesis, nanomaterial encapsulation, and forensic verification.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Sector Deployment}: Ready for integration across pharmaceuticals, petroleum, defense materials, and tax stamps.
    \item \textbf{Forensic Certainty}: Physical DNA markers bound to database records, providing absolute authentication.
\end{itemize}
