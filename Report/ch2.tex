\section{DNA Taggant Construct \& Bacterial Host}
The molecular marker developed for this project is a unique, synthetic 200~bp double-stranded DNA sequence designed specifically for covert industrial authentication. The construct was supplied cloned into the pUC57 plasmid vector with ampicillin selection (Lot No. BJ0335352-1, miniprep yield $\sim 4~\mu\text{g}$) and hosted in transformed \textit{Escherichia coli} (Stab Top10).

\section{Bacterial Culture \& Colony Heat-Lysis}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cultivation}: Transformed Stab Top10 \textit{E. coli} cells were cultured in 10~mL Luria-Bertani (LB) broth supplemented with ampicillin ($100~\mu\text{g/mL}$) in a shaking incubator at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for 16 hours. Solid cultures were maintained on ampicillin-selective LB agar plates.
    \item \textbf{Rapid Heat-Lysis}: Rather than utilizing commercial plasmid extraction kits, individual bacterial colonies were suspended in $100~\mu\text{L}$ of sterile TE buffer (10~mM Tris-HCl, 1~mM EDTA, pH~8.0). Heat lysis was executed in a thermal cycler at $95^\circ\text{C}$ for 10 minutes. Following a brief centrifugation, the supernatant was used directly as template for PCR amplification.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Universal Primers \& Sequence-Specific Probes}
Enzymatic amplification and real-time quantification utilize standardized universal primers and four sequence-specific fluorophore-labeled hydrolysis probes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Universal Forward Primer}: $T_m = 58.5^\circ\text{C}$ (reconstituted in $194.4~\mu\text{L}$ nuclease-free water for $100~\mu\text{M}$ stock).
    \item \textbf{Universal Reverse Primer}: Reconstituted in $180.4~\mu\text{L}$ nuclease-free water for $100~\mu\text{M}$ stock.
    \item \textbf{Sequence-Specific Optical Probes (A, B, C, D)}: Four distinct probes targeting internal regions of the 200~bp taggant construct (Figure~\ref{fig:physical_probes} and Table~\ref{tab:probe_specifications}).
\end{itemize}
