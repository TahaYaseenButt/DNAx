\section{Molecular Architecture \& Probe Allocation}
The synthetic DNA construct is engineered as a linear double-stranded DNA molecule. While the software platform supports arbitrary sequence lengths, a 200~bp linear construct was selected as the prototype for empirical laboratory validation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Universal Primer Docks (Flanking 20~bp Regions)}: Standardized forward and reverse primer binding sites flank the construct, allowing universal enzymatic amplification across any product batch without altering the master PCR master mix chemistry.
    \item \textbf{Multiplex Optical Probe Docks (Four 24~bp Regions)}: Four distinct sequence-specific probe regions are arrayed internally, each assigned to an optical channel:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Probe A}: FAM dye ($\lambda_{\text{em}} \approx 520\text{ nm}$, Quencher: BHQ1).
        \item \textbf{Probe B}: HEX dye ($\lambda_{\text{em}} \approx 556\text{ nm}$, Quencher: BHQ1).
        \item \textbf{Probe C}: Texas Red dye ($\lambda_{\text{em}} \approx 610\text{ nm}$, Quencher: BHQ2).
        \item \textbf{Probe D}: Cy5 dye ($\lambda_{\text{em}} \approx 670\text{ nm}$, Quencher: BHQ2).
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Neutral Spacer Segments}: Short, non-repetitive polynucleotide spacers separate the probe targets, eliminating steric crowding and preventing fluorescence quenching between adjacent fluorophores during simultaneous thermal binding.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Thermodynamic Assay Engineering}
To ensure robust, simultaneous amplification of all targets in a single reaction vessel, melting temperatures ($T_m$) are calculated using the unified SantaLucia nearest-neighbor thermodynamic model:
\begin{equation}
    T_m = \frac{\Delta H^\circ}{\Delta S^\circ + R \ln(C_T / 4)} + 16.6 \log_{10}[\text{Na}^+]
\end{equation}
where $\Delta H^\circ$ is enthalpy, $\Delta S^\circ$ is entropy, $R$ is the universal gas constant, $C_T$ is oligonucleotide concentration, and $[\text{Na}^+]$ is the monovalent cation concentration.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Primer Annealing Matching}: Forward and reverse primer melting temperatures are tightly balanced at $T_m = 58.5 \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ to avoid asymmetric extension rates.
    \item \textbf{Probe Thermal Offset ($\Delta T_m \ge +10.0^\circ\text{C}$)}: All internal fluorescent probes are engineered with $T_m \ge 68.0^\circ\text{C}$. This $+10.0^\circ\text{C}$ elevation guarantees that during the thermal cycler annealing phase, all fluorophore probes hybridize to their single-stranded targets \textit{prior} to primer binding and Taq polymerase 5'--3' exonucleolytic cleavage.
\end{itemize}

\section{Bio-Safety Filtering \& Offline BLAST}
Synthetic DNA taggants must be completely benign and environmentally inert:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Zero Pathogen Homology}: Generated sequences undergo automated BLASTn screening against local offline mirrors of the NCBI GenBank database (encompassing human, animal, plant, bacterial, and viral genomes). Any sequence sharing $>25\%$ homology or an $E\text{-value} < 10^{-2}$ is rejected.
    \item \textbf{Absence of Secondary Structures}: Folding algorithms filter out hairpins and self-dimers with $\Delta G < -3.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ to prevent self-priming.
    \item \textbf{GC Content Clamp}: Nucleotide composition is strictly constrained between $48\%\text{--}52\%$, with homopolymer runs prohibited ($>3$ consecutive identical nucleotides).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Industrial Product Integration}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Commercial Packaging \& 2D QR Codes}: The taggant spray was applied directly to printed 2D DataMatrix and QR code zones on pharmaceutical packaging (Macter syrup bottles, Bismol cartons, and tablet blister foils).
    \item \textbf{Direct Liquid Dosing}: Tagging solution was micro-dosed into liquid consumer products (\textit{Gelsemium 30, Surkhana}) at sub-ppb concentrations, preserving chemical stability, clarity, and pharmacological potency.
    \item \textbf{Automated Industrial Spraying}: Deployed via a 6-station roll-to-roll industrial spray machine equipped with infrared flash curing ($60^\circ\text{C}$, 1.5 seconds) for instant carrier solvent evaporation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Field Extraction \& Forensic Recovery}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Surface Swabbing}: A sterile cotton swab moistened with TE buffer scrubs the marked packaging or security stamp.
    \item \textbf{Chemical De-Shielding}: For silica-coated taggants, mild fluoride buffer dissolves the outer inorganic shell within 10 minutes.
    \item \textbf{Purification}: Standard silica membrane spin columns elute purified DNA in $25.0~\mu\text{L}$ nuclease-free water.
    \item \textbf{qPCR Verification}: Recovered eluate is tested in a 4-channel real-time thermal cycler using sequence-specific fluorophore probes (FAM, HEX, Texas Red, Cy5) to establish court-admissible forensic authentication.
\end{enumerate}
