\section{Phase I: In Silico Bioinformatic Assay Engineering}
The design phase is governed by the proprietary \textbf{DNAx Signet\textsuperscript{\textcopyright}} computational engine, executing multi-tiered algorithmic validations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Construct Sizing \& Structural Geometry}: Standardized to a 200~base pair (bp) linear double-stranded DNA construct. The core payload contains four modular 24~bp target domains flanked by 20~bp forward and reverse primer binding sites (Figure~\ref{fig:molecular_anatomy}).
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Optimization via SantaLucia Boundary Conditions}: Melting temperatures ($T_m$) are calculated using unified nearest-neighbor thermodynamic parameters:
    \begin{equation}
        T_m = \frac{\Delta H^\circ}{\Delta S^\circ + R \ln(C_T / 4)} + 16.6 \log_{10}[\text{Na}^+]
    \end{equation}
    Primers are constrained to $T_m = 58.5 \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ ($\Delta T_m \le 0.5^\circ\text{C}$ between forward and reverse), while multiplex probes are matched at $T_m \ge 68.0^\circ\text{C}$ (strictly $+10.0^\circ\text{C}$ differential above primers to ensure probe binding precedence).
    \item \textbf{Biological Safety and Local BLAST Screening}: Sequences are screened against offline NCBI genome mirrors to guarantee $<25\%$ homology with any known pathogenic or environmental organism, eliminating false-positive amplification and biosafety risks.
\end{itemize}

\section{Phase II: High-Fidelity Biomanufacturing \& Bulk Formulation}
Upon computational validation, the physical DNA construct is synthesized, amplified, and stabilized:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Master Plasmid Miniprep}: Physical synthesis was executed in high-copy pUC57 vector miniprep (Lot No. BJ033535).
    \item \textbf{High-Fidelity PCR Amplification}: Bulk thermal cycling yields milligram-scale quantities of analytical-grade 200~bp amplicon.
    \item \textbf{1.0-Liter Bulk Carrier Stabilization}: Amplified construct is blended into an industrial-grade aqueous carrier matrix ($10\text{ mL } 1\text{M Tris-HCl}, 2\text{ mL } 0.5\text{M EDTA at pH } 8.0, 988\text{ mL }\text{ddH}_2\text{O}, 100~\mu\text{L}\text{ PCR product}$), generating 1.0~Liter of stabilized bulk taggant solution ready for plant distribution.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Phase III: Industrial Substrate Dispersion \& Automated Application}
The stabilized taggant is deployed into physical industrial products:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Direct Liquid Dosing}: Integrated into liquid pharmaceuticals (\textit{Gelsemium 30, Surkhana}) and high-value chemical products at non-toxic parts-per-billion concentrations.
    \item \textbf{Automated Packaging Spray Machine}: Engineered inline 6-station roll-to-roll spray and infrared curing assembly line operating at $60^\circ\text{C}$ (1.5-second flash dry) for high-speed carton and label printing.
\end{itemize}

\section{Phase IV: Forensic Extraction \& Field Sampling Protocols}
In enforcement and audit scenarios, field officers execute non-destructive recovery:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Field Swabbing}: A sterile swab wetted with extraction buffer captures surface-bound taggants.
    \item \textbf{Silica Shell Chemical Etching}: For encapsulated formulations, buffered fluoride ($\text{NH}_4\text{F}/\text{HF}$) dissolves outer inorganic matrices within 10 minutes.
    \item \textbf{Column Chromatography}: Spin-column membrane isolation yields purified, amplifiable DNA template in $25.0~\mu\text{L}$ nuclease-free water.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Phase V: Optical Real-Time Multiplex Verification \& Certification}
Purified template is interrogated in a 4-channel real-time optical thermal cycler:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Simultaneous 4-Plex Optical Interrogation}: Probes labeled with FAM ($520\text{ nm}$), HEX ($556\text{ nm}$), Texas Red ($610\text{ nm}$), and Cy5 ($670\text{ nm}$) generate distinct fluorescence trajectories.
    \item \textbf{Cryptographic Authentication}: The recorded optical signature is queried against the national central database ledger, generating an unforgeable, court-admissible \textbf{Forensic Certificate of Authenticity}.
\end{itemize}
