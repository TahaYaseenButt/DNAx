\chapter{Empirical Validation: Wet-Lab Synthesis \& Multiplex Detection}
\label{chap:empirical_validation}

\section{Overview of Wet-Lab Experimental Proof-of-Concept}
To definitively validate the technological feasibility of the national tagging architecture, complete wet-lab experimental trials were executed at the ASAB-NUST molecular biology research facility. The trials achieved two major scientific milestones:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Construct Sizing \& Amplicon Purity}: Physical synthesis and electrophoretic verification of the $200\text{ bp}$ master DNA construct with zero non-specific amplification.
    \item \textbf{4-Channel Real-Time Multiplex qPCR Verification}: Simultaneous, unambiguous optical detection across all four target fluorophores (FAM, HEX, Texas Red, Cy5) in a single closed-tube reaction vessel.
\end{enumerate}

The electrophoretic analysis conclusively proved:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Construct Sizing Fidelity}: The amplified DNA migrated precisely at the $200\text{ bp}$ marker band, confirming that no truncation or insertion occurred during physical synthesis.
    \item \textbf{High Reaction Purity}: Absence of smear degradation or secondary bands verified 100\% primer specificity.
    \item \textbf{Negative Control Baseline}: Lane 3 (No Template Control) showed zero background signal, confirming pristine reagent purity.
\end{itemize}
