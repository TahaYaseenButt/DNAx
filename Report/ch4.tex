\chapter{Experimental Results}
\label{chap:results}

\section{Overview}
Wet-lab experiments conducted at ASAB-NUST successfully achieved:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item High-fidelity PCR synthesis and electrophoretic confirmation of the pure 200~bp prototype construct.
    \item Real-time multiplex qPCR detection with 100\% optical signal across all 4 probe channels (FAM, HEX, Texas Red, Cy5) in a single reaction.
\end{enumerate}
