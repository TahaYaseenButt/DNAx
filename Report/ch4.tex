\chapter{Experimental Results}
\label{chap:results}

\section{Overview}
Wet-lab experiments conducted at ASAB-NUST successfully achieved:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Rapid colony PCR recovery ($95^\circ\text{C}$, 10~min heat-lysis) and gradient PCR optimization ($57^\circ\text{C}$) of the 200~bp prototype construct.
    \item Validation of 1.0-Liter bulk spray formulation homogeneity ($C_q \approx 4.2\text{--}5.7$ across 5 random fractions).
    \item Real-time multiplex qPCR detection with 100\% optical signal across all 4 probe channels (FAM, HEX, Texas Red, Cy5) in a single reaction.
\end{enumerate}
