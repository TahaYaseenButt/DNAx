\section*{Annexure C: Contractually Approved Project Deliverables and 1-Year Timeline}
Table~\ref{tab:contractual_milestones} outlines the official milestone schedule approved in the original funded project proposal.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\caption*{Contractual Milestone Schedule (1-Year Project Trajectory)}
\label{tab:contractual_milestones}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l p{4.5cm} p{7.5cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Month} & \textbf{Milestone / Activity} & \textbf{Operational Deliverable Description} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Month 1} & Project Initiation \& Planning & Finalize project team, secure institutional permits, establish formal procurement workflows. \\
\textbf{Month 2} & Procurement \& Lab Setup & Purchase laboratory instruments, install surveillance, setup baseline data systems. \\
\textbf{Month 3} & Training \& Capacity Building & Train research staff, establish standard operating procedures (SOPs), finalize protocols. \\
\textbf{Month 4--5} & Field Sampling \& Physical Tagging & Initiate tag application trials on packaging materials, security labels, and metallic coupons. \\
\textbf{Month 6} & Initial Lab Analysis & Extract applied DNA constructs, perform gel electrophoresis, validate molecular stability. \\
\textbf{Month 7--8} & Field Sampling Phase II & Collect stressed/aged field samples, conduct multi-substrate environmental surveys. \\
\textbf{Month 9} & Advanced Lab Analysis & Execute high-throughput qPCR multiplex runs, optimize fluorophore deconvolution. \\
\textbf{Month 10} & Bioinformatics \& DMS Integration & Data cleaning, genetic profiling, clustering, deployment of centralized DMS. \\
\textbf{Month 11} & Reporting \& Outreach & Draft comprehensive technical reports, engage industrial stakeholders and security agencies. \\
\textbf{Month 12} & Final Review \& Project Closeout & Final financial audits, technical audits, handover of completed DNAx digital and physical ecosystem. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
