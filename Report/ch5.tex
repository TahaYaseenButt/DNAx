\section{Nano-Shielding Rationale}
For high-stress applications — such as metallic projectile casting, pyrotechnics, intense UV radiation, or chemical washing — synthetic DNA is protected inside an inorganic core-shell nanoparticle (Figure~\ref{fig:core_shell_architecture}).

        % Secondary Silica & Phosphor Shell
        \draw[line width=0.9pt, fill=gray!10, draw=black] (0,0) circle (2.8cm);

        \node[font=\footnotesize\bfseries, color=black] at (0, 1.75) {Synthetic DNA Layer ($\delta^-$ Backbone)};
