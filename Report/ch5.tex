\section{Nano-Shielding Rationale: Thermal, Chemical, and Mechanical Defense}
While bare synthetic DNA is stable in buffered liquid carriers, harsh industrial environments — such as metallic projectile casting, high-temperature pyrotechnics, explosive detonations, intense UV solar irradiation, and corrosive chemical exposures — demand robust physical protection. To overcome these constraints, an inorganic multi-layer core-shell nanoparticle system was engineered (Figure~\ref{fig:core_shell_architecture}).
