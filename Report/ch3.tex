\section{1.0-Liter Bulk Carrier Formulation in TE Buffer}
To enable taggant application, a 1.0-Liter carrier formulation was prepared using sterile Tris-EDTA (TE) buffer (10~mM Tris-HCl, 1~mM EDTA, pH~8.0). TE buffer maintains DNA stability by chelating divalent metal ions (such as $\text{Mg}^{2+}$).

The master formulation recipe is illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:bulk_1L_recipe_img} and summarized in Table~\ref{tab:bulk_1L_formulation}.

\subsection{Preparation \& Homogenization Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Buffer Preparation}: $10.0\text{ mL}$ of $1\text{ M Tris-HCl}$ and $2.0\text{ mL}$ of $0.5\text{ M EDTA}$ were added to $900\text{ mL}$ of distilled water, adjusted to $\text{pH} = 8.0$, and topped up to $1000\text{ mL}$.
    \item \textbf{Autoclaving}: The buffer was sterilized in an autoclave at $121^\circ\text{C}$ for 20 minutes and cooled to room temperature.
    \item \textbf{Taggant Dosing}: $100.0~\mu\text{L}$ of amplified 200~bp DNA taggant product (pooled from 4--5 tubes of PCR reactions) was introduced into the 1.0-Liter container.
    \item \textbf{Homogenization}: The container was mixed on a laboratory blood mixer to achieve uniform spatial distribution throughout the 1.0-Liter volume.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Application \& Recovery on Packaging QR Codes}
The formulated 1.0-Liter DNA taggant solution was applied and recovered from commercial packaging materials:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Application}: The taggant was applied over printed 2D QR codes and barcodes on commercial pharmaceutical packaging surfaces (cartons, bottles, and blister foils).
    \item \textbf{Recovery Protocol}: A sterile cotton swab moistened with sterile TE buffer was firmly swabbed across the marked QR code surface, and eluted in a microcentrifuge tube for direct qPCR verification.
\end{itemize}
