\section{1.0-Liter Bulk Carrier Formulation in TE Buffer}
To enable industrial-scale application across commercial products, a standardized 1.0-Liter carrier formulation was prepared using sterile Tris-EDTA (TE) buffer (10~mM Tris-HCl, 1~mM EDTA, pH~8.0). TE buffer maintains long-term DNA integrity by chelating divalent metal ions (such as $\text{Mg}^{2+}$) that serve as co-factors for environmental DNases.

\subsection{Sterilization \& Mixing Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Buffer Preparation}: $10.0\text{ mL}$ of $1\text{ M Tris-HCl}$ and $2.0\text{ mL}$ of $0.5\text{ M EDTA}$ were measured and added to $900\text{ mL}$ of distilled water. The solution was adjusted to $\text{pH} = 8.0$ and brought to a total volume of $1000\text{ mL}$.
    \item \textbf{Autoclaving}: The carrier buffer was sterilized in an autoclave at $121^\circ\text{C}$ for 20 minutes to eliminate any biological contaminants and allowed to cool to ambient temperature.
    \item \textbf{Taggant Dosing}: $100.0~\mu\text{L}$ of amplified 200~bp DNA taggant product (pooled from 4--5 tubes of endpoint PCR reactions) was introduced into the 1.0-Liter container.
    \item \textbf{Homogenization}: The container was agitated on a laboratory blood mixer for continuous rotational blending, achieving uniform spatial distribution of the DNA molecules throughout the 1.0-Liter volume.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Application to Commercial Products \& Packaging}
The formulated 1.0-Liter DNA taggant spray was tested across diverse commercial packaging surfaces and liquid formulations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pharmaceutical Packaging \& Blister Foils}: Applied directly to commercial pharmaceutical packaging materials, including Macter syrup bottles, Bismol cartons, and tablet blister foils.
    \item \textbf{2D QR Code Integration}: The taggant was applied over printed 2D DataMatrix and QR code zones on product labels, associating the physical bio-molecular tag with digital identification strings.
    \item \textbf{Direct Liquid Micro-Dosing}: Trace quantities of the taggant solution were introduced into liquid consumer formulations (\textit{Gelsemium 30, Surkhana}) at parts-per-billion concentrations, preserving chemical clarity, physical stability, and product potency.
\end{itemize}

\section{Field Sampling \& Forensic Swabbing Protocol}
To authenticate tagged products in the field without destroying packaging:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A sterile cotton swab moistened with sterile TE buffer is firmly rubbed across the marked QR code or packaging surface.
    \item The swab head is immersed in $25.0\text{--}50.0~\mu\text{L}$ of sterile nuclease-free water or TE buffer in a microcentrifuge tube.
    \item The eluted liquid is used directly as template ($1.0\text{--}2.0~\mu\text{L}$) for confirmatory real-time qPCR forensic deconvolution.
\end{enumerate}
