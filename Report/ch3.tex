\chapter{Physical DNA Synthesis, PCR Amplification \& Bulk Formulation}
\label{chap:synthesis}

\section{Introduction and Chemical Synthesis}
Following the in silico design and thermodynamic verification in DNAx Signet\textsuperscript{\textcopyright}, physical synthesis of the validated 200~bp sequence was initiated. The complete double-stranded DNA construct was commercially cloned into a high-copy cloning vector (\textbf{pUC57-Amp}) under \textbf{Lot No. BJ033535} and purified via standard silica spin-column miniprep. 

In parallel, high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC)-purified single-stranded DNA primers and dual-labeled fluorophore/quencher TaqMan probes were acquired for amplification and validation assays:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Forward Primer}: 20~nt ($T_m \approx 59.2^\circ\text{C}$, $\text{GC} = 50.0\%$).
    \item \textbf{Reverse Primer}: 20~nt ($T_m \approx 58.8^\circ\text{C}$, $\text{GC} = 50.0\%$).
    \item \textbf{Probe A (FAM Channel)}: 24~nt, $5'\text{-FAM}$ reporter, $3'\text{-BHQ-1}$ dark quencher ($T_m = 69.5^\circ\text{C}$).
    \item \textbf{Probe B (HEX Channel)}: 24~nt, $5'\text{-HEX}$ reporter, $3'\text{-BHQ-1}$ dark quencher ($T_m = 70.1^\circ\text{C}$).
    \item \textbf{Probe C (Texas Red / ROX Channel)}: 24~nt, $5'\text{-Texas Red}$ reporter, $3'\text{-BHQ-2}$ dark quencher ($T_m = 69.8^\circ\text{C}$).
    \item \textbf{Probe D (Cy5 Channel)}: 24~nt, $5'\text{-Cy5}$ reporter, $3'\text{-BHQ-3}$ dark quencher ($T_m = 70.4^\circ\text{C}$).
\end{itemize}

\section{Agarose Gel Electrophoresis Verification}
Amplified PCR products were resolved on a $2.0\%$ (w/v) high-resolution agarose gel prepared in $1\times\text{ TAE}$ buffer (40~mM Tris-acetate, 1~mM EDTA, pH 8.3) pre-stained with ethidium bromide ($0.5~\mu\text{g/mL}$). A 100~bp DNA molecular weight ladder was electrophoresed in parallel at $90\text{ V}$ for 45 minutes and visualized under ultraviolet transillumination ($302\text{ nm}$).

No non-specific high-molecular-weight amplicons or lower primer-dimer bands were detected, confirming complete sequence specificity and optimal primer annealing thermodynamics.

\subsection{Formulation Protocol for 1~Liter of Taggant Buffer}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{10.0~mL of 1.0~M Tris-HCl (pH 8.0)}: Acts as a primary biological pH buffer, maintaining ionic stability in the range of $\text{pH } 7.8\text{--}8.2$.
    \item \textbf{2.0~mL of 0.5~M EDTA (pH 8.0)}: Serves as a potent divalent cation chelator ($\text{Mg}^{2+}, \text{Ca}^{2+}$), effectively inactivating trace environmental deoxyribonucleases (DNases) that could otherwise catalyze enzymatic degradation of the taggant.
    \item \textbf{988.0~mL of Ultra-Pure Sterile Distilled Water ($\text{ddH}_2\text{O}$)}.
    \item \textbf{100.0~$\mu\text{L}$ of Concentrated PCR Amplicon Product} (derived from 4--5 bulk PCR tubes, yielding an estimated working copy number of $\sim 10^7\text{--}10^8\text{ copies/mL}$).
\end{enumerate}

The resulting formulation provides an optically transparent, stable aqueous taggant that can be directly metered into printing inks, adhesives, industrial coating suspensions, and liquid pharmaceutical lots without altering the physicochemical properties of the host product.
