\section{Introduction and Strategic Context}
Product counterfeiting, illicit trade, and unauthorized tampering pose significant risks to industrial supply chains, consumer safety, and commercial brand integrity. Critical sectors—including pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, fuels, and consumer packaged goods—frequently suffer from fraudulent reproduction and substitution.

Conventional security mechanisms, such as overt holograms, standard serial numbers, and 2D barcodes, authenticate only the outer packaging surface. Because these optical markers can be physically copied, photographed, or transferred, they fail to provide definitive, tamper-proof security.

To address these challenges, the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) initiated the \textbf{Indigenous DNA Tagging System for Industries}. By utilizing trace quantities of a unique, synthetic DNA construct as a covert molecular marker, products can be authenticated with forensic certainty using polymerase chain reaction (PCR) and real-time quantitative PCR (qPCR) detection.

\section{Specific Objectives of the Project}
The primary objective of this project phase is to establish a functional, reproducible laboratory workflow for the production, bulk formulation, product application, and forensic detection of an indigenous DNA taggant:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Construct Recovery and Bacterial Propagation}: To culture and recover the ordered 200~bp synthetic DNA taggant construct supplied in transformed \textit{Escherichia coli} (Stab Top10) in pUC57 plasmid vector.
    \item \textbf{PCR Amplification and Gel Verification}: To optimize enzymatic amplification conditions using universal primer pairs and verify construct size on 2.0\% agarose gel electrophoresis.
    \item \textbf{Serial Dilution Optimization}: To evaluate serial 1:10 dilutions ($D_1$ to $D_4$) of the amplicon, determine the optimal working dilution, and resolve template-dependent qPCR inhibition.
    \item \textbf{1.0-Liter Bulk Carrier Formulation}: To prepare a standardized 1.0-Liter formulation of stabilized Tris-EDTA (TE) buffer containing the micro-dosed DNA taggant suitable for industrial application.
    \item \textbf{Spatial Homogeneity Assessment}: To verify uniform distribution and stability of the DNA taggant across multiple fractions (F1 to F7) of the 1.0-Liter bulk preparation using real-time qPCR.
    \item \textbf{Sequence-Specific Probe Optimization}: To evaluate four distinct fluorophore probes (Probes A, B, C, and D) in individual and 4-plex multiplex real-time qPCR formats.
    \item \textbf{Commercial Product Application}: To apply the formulated DNA taggant onto industrial packaging materials (syrup bottles, cartons, blister foils) associated with 2D QR codes, as well as liquid formulations, and demonstrate forensic recovery via surface swabbing.
\end{enumerate}
