\section{Introduction}
Product counterfeiting and unauthorized tampering pose risks to industrial supply chains, consumer safety, and brand integrity. Conventional security mechanisms, such as surface barcodes and holograms, can be duplicated or physically detached.

To address these challenges, the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) developed an \textbf{Indigenous DNA Tagging System for Industries}. Using trace quantities of a synthetic DNA construct as a molecular marker, products and packaging can be verified with high specificity using polymerase chain reaction (PCR) and real-time quantitative PCR (qPCR) detection.

\section{Project Objectives}
The specific objectives completed in this work include:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Construct Recovery}: Culture and recover the 200~bp synthetic DNA taggant construct supplied in transformed \textit{Escherichia coli} (Stab Top10) in pUC57 plasmid vector.
    \item \textbf{PCR Amplification and Gel Verification}: Optimize enzymatic amplification conditions using universal primer pairs and verify construct size on 2.0\% agarose gel electrophoresis.
    \item \textbf{Serial Dilution Optimization}: Evaluate serial 1:10 dilutions ($D_1$ to $D_4$) of the amplicon to establish optimal working concentrations for qPCR detection.
    \item \textbf{1.0-Liter Bulk Carrier Formulation}: Prepare a standardized 1.0-Liter formulation of stabilized Tris-EDTA (TE) buffer containing the micro-dosed DNA taggant.
    \item \textbf{Spatial Homogeneity Assessment}: Verify uniform distribution of the DNA taggant across multiple fractions (F1 to F7) of the 1.0-Liter bulk preparation using real-time qPCR.
    \item \textbf{Sequence-Specific Probe Verification}: Evaluate four distinct fluorophore probes (Probes A, B, C, and D) in individual and 4-plex multiplex real-time qPCR formats.
    \item \textbf{Packaging QR Code Application \& Recovery}: Apply the formulated taggant onto commercial packaging materials over 2D QR codes and demonstrate recovery via surface swabbing.
    \item \textbf{5-Software Digital Ecosystem}: Develop an integrated 5-module digital management system (\textit{DNAx Signet}, \textit{DNAx Manufacture}, \textit{DNAx Inventory}, \textit{DNAx Vendor}, and \textit{DNAx Forensics}) connected via a central database to manage taggant design, product integration, inventory tracking, distribution checks, and laboratory verification.
\end{enumerate}
